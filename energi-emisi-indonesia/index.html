Data. Electricity generation, power-sector emissions and carbon intensity: Ember, Yearly Electricity Data (CC BY 4.0). Emissions by sector: Climate Watch (WRI), via Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0). Downloaded .

Notes. Ember uses life-cycle emission factors, so renewables show small non-zero emissions. "Bioenergy & geothermal" combines Ember's bioenergy and "other renewables" (mostly geothermal here). Land use change & forestry emissions are large and uncertain and move in steps, so they are hidden by default. ASEAN totals are not available in the sector data. The what-if panel holds demand at the latest year, replaces coal one-for-one with renewables at the country's own average factors, and ignores grid, storage and cost.
